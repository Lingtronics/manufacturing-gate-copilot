# Fictional Validation Sprint charter

**Hypothetical consulting proposal. No actual engagement, endorsement or achieved savings.**

## Business objective and sponsor

Help fictional Northstar reduce avoidable Gate 3 preparation effort and trace requirements, unresolved defects and validation to approved evidence. Launch decisions remain with customer leaders.

Sponsor persona: fictional VP of Manufacturing Operations, accountable for pilot readiness and capacity. Quality owns gate policy, Engineering owns validation, Operations owns work readiness, and Programme Management coordinates review/actions.

## Opportunity and value baseline

Consolidated evidence may reduce collection/preparation effort if adoption and source quality support it. Synthetic baseline: 40 collection hours + 24 preparation hours per gate, 12 annual reviews, GBP 65/hour, 15-day cycle, 60% evidence coverage. Defect recurrence is unknown and excluded from finance. These are inputs to validate, not real observations.

## Scope, exclusions and access assumptions

Scope: one programme/Gate 3, approved source mapping, quality/change/action traceability, deterministic assessment, grounded explanation, human review, approved collaboration export, baseline measurement and scenario validation.

Exclude launch authorisation, certified safety assessment, autonomous defect closure, unrestricted enterprise ingestion, replacing PLM/QMS/MES, production identity rollout and unsupported defect/revenue benefits.

Named owners supply approved synthetic or authorised pilot extracts with stable IDs and policy/revision ownership. Collaboration rights and destination must be approved. Sensitive production/personal data is unnecessary. Live Miro access is optional and separately authorised.

## Exit criteria and success metrics

- Each requirement has verified evidence or an explicit gap.
- Every safety-rule golden case passes and model outputs cannot override blockers.
- Human owners agree findings reflect supplied records and identify remaining policy/data gaps.
- Repeat publication has no duplicate managed items and preserves unrelated content.
- Collection/preparation effort is measured consistently; improvement claims require actual comparable baseline/trial evidence.
- Financial outputs reproduce inputs and disclose adoption/costs; modelled and realised benefit remain distinct.
- Sponsor accepts limits, ownership and next scope. Sprint exit does not authorise production.

## Risks and proposed next implementation

Access/revision ambiguity requires owner reconciliation. Quality must validate criteria. Adoption uncertainty requires observed use and revised assumptions. Incorrect model claims require structured grounding and fallback. Collaboration needs explicit permission, synthetic scope and audit. Unavailable integration retains the tested mock path.

If hypothetical criteria are met: propose production SSO, controlled PLM/QMS adapters, revision stewardship, OAuth connection, monitored publication/recovery, measured adoption and approved retention/security. There are no actual customer sprint outcomes to report.
